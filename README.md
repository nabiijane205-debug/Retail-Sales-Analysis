# Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## Overview
An exploratory data analysis project built with Microsoft Power BI and DAX to understand retail sales, costs, profit, and product performance.

## Business Problem
A retail shop needs to identify its strongest products and regions, understand profitability, and make better inventory decisions.

## Tools
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

## Dashboard Features
- Total sales, cost, and profit KPI cards
- Profit margin analysis
- Sales by product and category
- Regional sales comparison
- Profit by product
- Interactive region slicer

## Key Findings
- Laptops generated the highest total sales.
- Nairobi generated the highest regional sales.
- Electronics outperformed accessories in sales and profit.
- Keyboards and mice had higher profit margins than laptops.

## Recommendations
- Consider prioritising laptop inventory while monitoring demand and costs.
- Investigate ways to increase accessory sales, including bundling accessories with electronics.
- Compare total profit and profit margin before making inventory decisions.

## Skills Practised
- Data entry and preparation
- Data visualisation
- Interactive filtering
- DAX measures
- Business insight development

## Dataset
A small sample retail dataset created for learning and demonstration purposes.

## Limitations
The dataset covers only six months and contains 12 sales records. The findings are illustrative and should not be treated as definitive business forecasts.
